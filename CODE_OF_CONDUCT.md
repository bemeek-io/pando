# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be civil. Argue about the work rather than the person doing it. Assume the other person is
reasonable until they have shown you otherwise, and say what you mean plainly enough that they can
disagree with it.

Specifically, these are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, or
anywhere else this project happens:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Insults, personal attacks, or comments about someone's identity — age, body, disability, ethnicity,
  sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socio-economic
  status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions without new information, or demanding unpaid work
  from maintainers.

Technical disagreement is not a violation of this document, and neither is a blunt review. "This is
wrong, and here is why" is the job. "You are an idiot" is not.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
place someone is representing the project. It also applies to behavior outside those spaces when it
affects someone's ability to take part in them.

## Reporting

Report a problem through [GitHub's private reporting
form](https://github.com/bemeek-io/pando/security/advisories/new) — the same channel as a security
report, because it is the one private channel this project has that is definitely monitored. Start
the report with "Code of conduct" so it is not triaged as a vulnerability. Reports are kept
confidential, and you will get an acknowledgment within three business days.

If the report concerns a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled by someone who is not the person
being reported on.

Include what happened, where, and when, with links if there are any. You do not need to have decided
whether something is a violation before reporting it.

## What happens next

Maintainers will read the report, talk to the people involved, and respond in proportion to what
happened. In order of severity:

1. **A private word.** An explanation of what was wrong and why, with a request to stop.
2. **A warning.** The same, on the record, with a stated consequence for continuing.
3. **A temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **A permanent ban.**

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith can expect the same consequences as anyone
else.
