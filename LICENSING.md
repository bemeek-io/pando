# Licensing

Pando is **dual-licensed**.

## Open source — AGPL-3.0

The default. The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). In short: you may use, modify and distribute
Pando, and if you run a modified version as a network service you must offer its users the
corresponding source (AGPL §13).

For most people this is the right licence and nothing else is needed — running Pando to host your own
apps, modifying it for yourself, or contributing back are all covered.

## Commercial

A commercial licence is available for cases where AGPL §13 does not suit: embedding Pando in a
proprietary product, or offering it as a hosted service without publishing your modifications.

Ask by opening an issue or contacting [bemeek-io](https://github.com/bemeek-io).

## Why this way round

AGPL → MIT is reversible; MIT → AGPL is not. Starting permissive forecloses the choice, so Pando
starts at AGPL and can be relaxed later if that turns out to be right. Nothing about the codebase
assumes either answer.

## Contributions

Contributions are accepted under the AGPL. Dual licensing means a contribution may also be offered
under the commercial licence, so by opening a pull request you agree that your contribution can be
distributed under both.
